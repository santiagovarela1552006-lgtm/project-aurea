The Problem

The real pain isn't discovery — it's transaction orchestration.

Buying a home in Spain is digital at the top of the funnel and fragmented everywhere else. Every step re-starts the same conversation with a new party.

Portal→Agent→Bank→ Lawyer→Notary→Insurance→Post-sale

Duplicated data

Clients re-enter the same information across agents, banks and lawyers.

Slow response loops

High-value transactions depend on fragmented email, calls and WhatsApp threads.

Low process visibility

Buyer and seller rarely have a real-time transaction cockpit.

Manual coordination

Agents spend time qualifying, chasing and scheduling instead of closing.

Implication: the first company to own the workflow can capture economics beyond commission — mortgage, legal, insurance, SaaS and post-sale management.

The Solution

Áurea coordinates the transaction, end-to-end.

A workflow platform — a Transaction OS — that turns a fragmented process into a single managed transaction.

Product

A transaction cockpit first — an operating system over time.

The MVP solves one painful workflow, then expands across the transaction stack.

01

Search, shortlist, AI valuation, mortgage calculator, alerts, document checklist.

02

Transaction orchestration

CRM, client qualification, scheduling, workflow status, partner handoffs.

03

Partner integrations

Mortgage matching, legal partners, notary coordination, insurance, property management.

04

Data & AI infrastructure

RAG, transaction data model, lead scoring, valuation support, workflow agents.

AI is the cost advantage — the moat is workflow data and distribution. Human-in-the-loop for legal, financial and negotiation-critical decisions, always.
