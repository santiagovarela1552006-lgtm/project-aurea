Who we are

Áurea is an early-stage, pre-incorporation project operated by Santiago Varela ("we," "us"). You can reach us at santiago.varela1552006@gmail.com.

What we collect

The only personal data we collect through this site is what you choose to give us: your email address and selected role if you submit the "Request Early Access" form, or whatever you include if you email us directly.

We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts anywhere on this site.

How the access-request form works

Submitting that form sends your email and selected role to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-delivery service, which forwards it directly to our inbox by email. We don't run our own server or database, FormSubmit is simply the delivery mechanism between the form and our email. We only use the data to respond to you, as described below. See FormSubmit's own privacy policy for how they handle data in transit.

The investor cockpit demo

Your shortlist, saved alerts, and checklist progress in the cockpit prototype are stored only in your own browser's local storage. That data never leaves your device, and we never see or receive it.

How we use your data

Solely to respond to your inquiry and, if you ask us to, to keep you updated on Áurea's progress. We do not use it for any other purpose, and we do not sell or share it with any third party.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA, GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold about you. Since the only data we hold is whatever you've emailed us, you can exercise these rights at any time by emailing santiago.varela1552006@gmail.com and asking us to delete your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Áurea develops. We'll update the date at the top of this page when we do.
